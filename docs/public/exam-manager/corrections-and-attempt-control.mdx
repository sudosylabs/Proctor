# Correct Live Content and Control Attempts

An open or paused Sitting can receive an urgent correction without changing
its identity or forcing candidates to rejoin. The correction creates another
immutable Revision and retargets only that Sitting.

<GovernedFigure asset="correction-acknowledgement-sequence" />

## Keep the Correction Boundary Narrow

A live correction may change instructions, ordered Exam Resources, and Browser
Policy. It cannot change Exam Policy, Starter Workspace, Execution Profile,
Class eligibility, or schedule. The operation verifies those protected digests
match the current Revision.

For Resource bytes, stage each addition or replacement against the exact
Sitting, base Revision, target kind, Manager, and expiry. Staging has no
candidate-visible effect. The final correction submits one complete ordered
manifest:

- omission removes a Resource;
- order becomes its new position;
- an item without a stage retains base content; and
- an item with a ready stage selects those bytes.

Failed or abandoned stages stay invisible and become retention-eligible.

## Write a Candidate-safe Notice

Every correction has a trimmed 1–500 character summary, canonically ordered
changed areas, and an explicit acknowledgement requirement. Keep the private
manager reason separate. Candidate notices must explain what changed without
revealing internal policy evaluation, another candidate, or an accusation.

When acknowledgement is required, each Attempt must acknowledge notices oldest
first. Pending acknowledgement blocks Workspace mutations, terminal use,
governed Browser navigation, and voluntary submission. It does not block
protected reads, Participation renewal, Focus Loss or Browser Activity
delivery, the acknowledgement itself, or authorized closure. The state belongs
to the Attempt and survives reconnect or re-allow.

## Distinguish Candidate Controls

| Control | What it means | What it preserves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kick | authorized manual interruption that blocks the active Attempt | Attempt, Workspace, evidence, and distinct manual provenance |
| automatic Suspension | policy or confirmed continuity enforcement | neutral evidence, Flag, triggering episode, and Workspace |
| re-allow | closes one exact Suspension episode and returns the Attempt to Ready | Flags, evidence, immutable configuration, and prior generations |
| manager end | seals any unfinished Ready, Active, or Suspended Attempt | last acknowledged Workspace and `manager_ended_attempt` Submission provenance |
| Sitting close | seals every unfinished created Attempt at deadline or early close | `sitting_closed` Submission provenance and prior continuity cause |

Re-allow requires the exact Suspension, expected Attempt revision, and a
private bounded reason. Fresh admission creates a new Participation generation;
old credentials cannot revive it. Reset only the triggering evaluation window,
so later violations can enforce again.

Manager end is terminal. It never reopens or resubmits an Attempt, and its
private reason is absent from candidate, mail, realtime, and Submission
projections. At or after the deadline, Sitting closure owns the otherwise
equivalent sealing transition.
