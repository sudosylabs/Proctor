# Keep Work and Continuity Acknowledged

The Attempt Workspace is remotely authoritative. A local editor buffer,
terminal filesystem, WebSocket write, or “saved” indicator is not durable until
the server acknowledges the exact mutation and Workspace Cursor.

## Work Inside the Protected Workspace

The Starter Workspace is copied logically into one private Attempt Workspace.
Exam Resources remain read-only outside it. Workspace paths are case-sensitive
POSIX-relative values and `.proctor` is reserved. Links, devices, sockets,
traversal, and recursive deletion of non-empty directories are rejected.

Each file has a stable Entry identity, mutable path, and opaque current Content
Version. Create, replace, move, and delete compare expected state and use an
Attempt-scoped idempotency key. After acknowledgement, retain the new Content
Version and Workspace Cursor.

An enabled Attempt Terminal is a non-authoritative projection of the same
Workspace. Guest changes become durable only after the server harvests,
validates, and acknowledges them. Ignored build/dependency trees and paths
outside the Workspace mount remain ephemeral and do not enter Submission.

## Separate Ping, Renewal, and Connection

<GovernedFigure asset="participation-lease-timeline" />

- WebSocket ping tests transport liveness only.
- Authenticated Participation renewal proves the generation and advances its
  PostgreSQL lease through a monotonic sequence.
- A Connection is one durable transport child of the current Participation.

Desktop schedules renewal at the server-provided interval. A duplicate sequence
returns its retained result; a stale sequence cannot extend the lease. Brief
reconnect before expiry may retain the generation. Expiry permanently fences
it, closes the Connection, records neutral Connection Loss evidence, and
suspends the Attempt.

One failed request or ping is not Connection Loss. Confirmed lease expiry is.
The candidate message should say secure continuity could not be renewed, not
accuse the candidate or reveal internal lease/generation vocabulary.

## Recover From Interruption

After a recoverable reconnect, refetch the presentation and reconcile the
ordered Workspace journal from the last acknowledged cursor. If history cannot
bridge the cursor, replace the local view from a complete manifest. Never push
an unacknowledged local tree over authoritative state.

After expiry, Kick, or automatic Suspension, only an authorized Manager can
re-allow the exact episode. Fresh admission creates a new Participation
generation; prior credentials, sequence numbers, and Connections remain fenced.
The same Attempt, Workspace, configuration, Flags, and evidence continue.
