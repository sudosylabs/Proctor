# Proctor Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the Proctor community welcoming, safe, and equitable for
everyone.

We are committed to an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and
contributions of every participant regardless of race, ethnicity, caste,
color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or
gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language,
philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position,
education, experience, or other status. The same opportunity to participate is
available to everyone acting in good faith under this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged Behavior

Community members are expected to act mindfully across differences in culture,
background, experience, and communication style. Healthy participation
includes:

1. respecting the community's purpose, activities, and spaces;
2. engaging kindly, honestly, and with empathy;
3. respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
4. giving and accepting constructive feedback;
5. taking responsibility for words, actions, and contributions;
6. apologizing, learning, and repairing harm when mistakes occur; and
7. acting in ways that sustain the well-being of the community.

## Restricted Behavior

The following conduct, including threats or encouragement of it, violates this
Code of Conduct:

1. harassment or continued personal attention after a clear request to stop;
2. insults, character attacks, trolling, or demeaning comments;
3. stereotyping, discrimination, or exclusion based on identity or immutable
   traits;
4. sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances inappropriate to the
   community;
5. sharing or acting on another person's private information without
   permission;
6. threats, encouragement of violence, or other endangerment;
7. impersonation or misleading identity used to deceive or evade enforcement;
8. failure to credit contributed or referenced work;
9. promotional or commercial material outside the purpose of the community;
10. irresponsible publication or description of harmful material; and
11. other conduct that would reasonably threaten the safety, dignity, or
    well-being of participants.

## Reporting an Incident

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. When conduct may have
crossed this boundary, report it privately and promptly by emailing
[support@sudosy.fr](mailto:support@sudosy.fr?subject=%5BProctor%20Code%20of%20Conduct%5D)
with the subject prefix `[Proctor Code of Conduct]`.

Include the relevant community space, approximate time, people involved,
available links or records, and any immediate safety concern. Do not publish
the report in an issue or other public channel. If the report concerns someone
who may have access to the mailbox, say so in the subject so a non-involved
maintainer can handle it.

The maintainers responsible for the affected Proctor community space act as
Community Moderators. They will review reports fairly, protect the privacy and
security of reporters and affected people, and limit details to those needed
to investigate and respond. Enforcement normally remains private, although a
broader communication may be part of a safe, mutually agreed resolution.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

Moderators will consider the impact, context, severity, and pattern of conduct.
They may skip steps when necessary to protect the community. The available
responses are:

1. **Warning.** A private written warning explains the violation and expected
   correction. Repair may include acknowledging responsibility, apologizing,
   or clarifying future expectations.
2. **Temporarily limited activity.** A time-limited restriction may apply to
   particular spaces or interactions after repeated or more serious conduct.
   Conditions for safe return will be stated privately.
3. **Temporary suspension.** Access to community spaces may be suspended for a
   defined period when warnings have not resolved a pattern or one incident is
   serious. Conditions for return may be imposed.
4. **Permanent ban.** Access may be permanently removed when other measures
   cannot keep the community safe or the violation is exceptionally severe.

This ladder guides rather than limits moderator judgment. Responses should be
proportionate, reasoned, consistent with community safety, and attentive to
repair where repair remains possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Proctor community spaces, including source
repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, review comments, project
email, events, and future official communication channels. It also applies when
someone officially represents Proctor, such as through an official email or
social-media account or as an appointed representative at an event.
